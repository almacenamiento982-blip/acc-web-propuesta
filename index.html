— About the tournament —

Where champions are crowned.

The Adidas Champions Cup returns to Tampa for its 4th edition: three days of high-level youth soccer, from the first whistle to the trophy stage.

Teams compete at the Tournament Sportsplex of Tampa Bay, one of Florida's premier multi-sport facilities, with a points system that keeps every game meaningful.

  • 3 games minimum, guaranteed
  • 1st & 2nd place awards
  • Open to boys and girls
  • Professional tournament management
  • Points system & real competition
  • Athletic trainer on site
